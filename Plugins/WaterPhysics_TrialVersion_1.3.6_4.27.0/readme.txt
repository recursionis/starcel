Place the contents of this in either 
    "UE4_PROJECT_ROOT/Plugins/WaterPhysics"
or
    "UE4_ROOT/Engine/Plugins/Marketplace/WaterPhysics"
    (UE4_ROOT is most commonly found in "C:/Program Files/Epic Games/UE_4.XX" on windows)

NOTE: The subdirectory "WaterPhysics" can be named anything you like. The only requirement is that the contents of this folder are placed directly within that directory.
